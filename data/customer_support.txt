CUSTOMER SUPPORT AND PAYMENT POLICY

Failed Payments:
If a customer is charged but an order is not confirmed,
the support team should investigate the transaction.
Customers should provide their transaction reference.

Refunds:
Approved refunds are normally processed within five to
seven business days. The actual time may depend on the
customer's bank or payment provider.

Account Recovery:
Customers who cannot access their accounts should use
the password recovery process. If they no longer have
access to their registered phone number or email address,
they should contact customer support for identity verification.

Support Escalation:
Unresolved payment issues should be escalated to the
payments team. Support agents should include the transaction
reference and relevant investigation notes.

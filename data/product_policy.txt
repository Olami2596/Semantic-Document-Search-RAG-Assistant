PRODUCT POLICY

Product Availability:

Product availability may change depending on stock levels, supplier availability, and operational conditions. Customers should check the product listing before placing an order. A product displayed on the website may occasionally become unavailable before an order is completed.

If a product becomes unavailable after an order has been placed, the customer should be notified. Support agents should explain the situation and provide available alternatives where possible. Customers may choose to wait for the product to become available again or request a cancellation and applicable refund.

Product Descriptions and Specifications:

Product descriptions, images, prices, and specifications are provided to help customers make informed purchasing decisions. Customers should review the product details carefully before completing an order.

Although reasonable efforts are made to ensure that product information is accurate, occasional errors may occur. These may include incorrect descriptions, outdated images, inaccurate specifications, or pricing discrepancies.

If a customer identifies incorrect product information, the issue should be reported to customer support. Support agents should document the affected product, the incorrect information, and the customer's concerns. Confirmed errors should be forwarded to the product management team for correction.

Returns and Replacements:

Customers who receive damaged, defective, or incorrect products should contact customer support to report the issue. They may be required to provide their order number, a description of the problem, and supporting photographs where applicable.

Returned products may be inspected to determine whether they qualify for a refund or replacement. Products should generally be returned in their original packaging and condition, together with any accessories supplied.

Return requests must be reviewed in accordance with the applicable return conditions. Certain products may be excluded from returns because of their nature, condition, or applicable restrictions. Support agents should explain any relevant restrictions before advising customers on the next steps.

Product Warranty and Defective Items:

Some products may include a manufacturer's warranty covering specific defects or performance issues. Warranty coverage, duration, and conditions may vary depending on the product and manufacturer.

Customers experiencing problems with a product should contact support with their order details and a description of the fault. Support agents should verify whether the product is covered by a warranty before advising on repair, replacement, or other available options.

Damage caused by misuse, unauthorised modifications, accidents, or improper maintenance may not be covered by the manufacturer's warranty.

Product Complaints and Support Escalation:

Customers who are dissatisfied with a product, its quality, or the service received should contact customer support. Support agents should record the customer's complaint, the affected product, the order reference, and any relevant evidence.

Complaints involving product quality, repeated defects, inaccurate specifications, or supplier-related issues should be escalated to the product management team.

The escalation notes should include the investigation findings, actions already taken, and any outstanding concerns. Customers should receive updates when additional investigation is required.

All product-related complaints should be handled professionally, with clear communication and appropriate follow-up until the issue is resolved.

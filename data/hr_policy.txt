EMPLOYEE BENEFITS AND HR POLICY

Annual Leave:
Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid annual
leave every year. Employees should submit leave requests to
their managers at least two weeks before the intended leave.

Sick Leave:
Employees who are unable to work due to illness should
notify their managers as soon as possible. A medical
certificate may be required for extended absences.

Remote Work:
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week,
subject to their manager's approval. Some roles may require
employees to work from the office more frequently.

Employee Resignation:
Employees who wish to leave the company should provide
one month's written notice. The HR department coordinates
the exit process and final documentation.

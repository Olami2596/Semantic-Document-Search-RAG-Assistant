ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN HEALTHCARE

Medical Imaging:
Artificial intelligence can help medical professionals
analyse X-rays, CT scans, and MRI images. Machine learning
models can identify patterns that may indicate disease.

Clinical Decision Support:
AI systems can assist healthcare professionals by analysing
patient information and highlighting potential risks.
These systems support clinical decisions but should not
replace qualified medical judgement.

Privacy:
Healthcare organisations must protect patient information.
Data should be handled securely, and personally identifiable
information should be protected during processing.

Predictive Analytics:
Predictive models can analyse historical patient data to
estimate the likelihood of certain health outcomes.
